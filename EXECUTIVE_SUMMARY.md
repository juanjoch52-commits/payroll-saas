# 📊 PayrollHub - Executive Summary

## The Opportunity

Small businesses (5-50 employees) in construction, remodeling, automotive, and food service **waste 3+ hours per week on payroll**.

Current solutions:
- **Spreadsheets:** Error-prone, time-consuming, no compliance
- **Enterprise software (ADP, Gusto):** $500+/month, overkill, complex
- **Manual calculation:** Incorrect taxes, compliance risks

## The Solution

**PayrollHub** - A simple, affordable SaaS payroll platform designed specifically for small business owners who need to "just get it done."

### Key Differentiators
1. **Price** - $50-100/month vs. $500+ for competitors
2. **Simplicity** - Set up in 15 minutes, not 3 weeks
3. **Bilingual** - Built for Hispanic business owners (20% of US small biz)
4. **Accuracy** - Automated federal tax calculations (never wrong)
5. **Focus** - Designed for micro businesses, not enterprises
