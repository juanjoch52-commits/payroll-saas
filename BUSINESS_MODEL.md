# 💼 Business Model & Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Market

### Primary: Small Businesses (5-50 employees)
- **Construction & Remodeling** - Contractors, construction managers
- **Automotive** - Mechanics, auto repair shops
- **Restaurants** - Cafés, small dining establishments
- **Service Industries** - Plumbers, electricians, etc.

### Why These Industries?
1. ✅ High labor costs (main expense)
2. ✅ High employee turnover (need frequent payroll runs)
3. ✅ Limited tech adoption (currently using spreadsheets)
4. ✅ Manual payroll processing (pain point)
5. ✅ Biweekly/weekly pay periods (more payroll processing)

### Customer Profile (Persona)

**Juan - Restaurant Owner**
- Age: 42
- Business: Small restaurant (12 employees)
- Current Setup: Excel spreadsheets + manual tax calculation
- Pain Points:
  - Spends 3 hours/week on payroll
  - Makes calculation errors
  - Doesn't understand federal tax withholding
  - Compliance concerns
- Needs: Simple, bilingual payroll tool that "just works"
